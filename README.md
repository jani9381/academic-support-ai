\# Academic Support AI



\## YottaFlex AI Forward Deployed Engineer Hackathon



An end-to-end Academic Support AI system for routing student and faculty requests, retrieving policy-grounded answers, calculating SLAs, detecting escalation requirements, preserving event traceability, and evaluating workflow performance.



\---



\## 1. Problem Statement



Academic institutions receive requests from students and faculty across multiple domains such as:



\- Financial Aid

\- Registration

\- IT Support

\- Accessibility



The system must determine the correct support team, retrieve applicable policy or knowledge sources, provide a grounded response, calculate the appropriate SLA, identify escalation requirements, and preserve traceability throughout the workflow.
